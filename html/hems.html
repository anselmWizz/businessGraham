About Us

Hi! I'm Marites Belmonte, the owner of Belmonte’s Graham Bar.

It all started with simple curiosity. One day, while scrolling through Facebook, I came across a tutorial on how to make graham bars. I decided to try it at home, and to my surprise, my family and friends loved it!

That’s when the idea came to turn it into a small business. Not just for extra income, but to share something sweet and homemade with others. ❤️

Every bite of our graham bar is made with love, care, and that comforting home-style sweetness you’ll always crave.
